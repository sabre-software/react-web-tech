# Risks and decisions

## Current risks

- The scaffolded project has no documented production ownership or deployment target.
- Framework and package versions may become stale if the exercise becomes a long-lived course asset.
- The existing navigation is instructional and is not a substitute for application routing.

## Decision log

- Keep the exercise unchanged while documenting its observed state.
- Defer modernization and productization until a named owner approves their learning value.
